Privacy Policy

How Morocco Wedding Guide handles account information, community reviews, photos, reports and technical data.

Pre-launch status. Browsing is public and the production account backend is not connected yet. We will not activate account-based contributions until the production data controller contact, CNDP formalities and backend details are completed.

1. Who controls your data

Morocco Wedding Guide is an independent wedding discovery project based in Morocco. Before production account features are enabled, this page will be updated with the legal identity and a working privacy contact for the data controller.

2. Data we may collect

  • Browsing: ordinary server/request data generated by hosting and media delivery, such as IP address, browser/device information and requested pages.
  • Language preference: your chosen language may be stored locally in your browser.
  • Account: email address, account identifier and authentication/session data when passwordless sign-in is activated.
  • Reviews: rating, review text, display name, provider reviewed, account identifier and submission time.
  • Photos: uploaded image, caption, provider, uploader account and moderation status.
  • Reports: provider, reason, optional details, reporter account and moderation status.

3. Why we use it

We use data only to operate secure accounts, publish moderated community feedback, prevent abuse, handle corrections/reports, maintain the directory, protect the service and respond to rights requests. We do not sell personal data.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where consent is required, we ask for it clearly. Account and contribution data is processed to provide the requested community features and to protect the integrity of the platform. Marketing will not be sent without the required consent and an opt-out mechanism.

5. Service providers and international hosting

The public pilot is hosted on GitHub Pages. Current decorative images are delivered by Unsplash and Pexels. When accounts are activated, Supabase is planned for authentication, database and private photo storage. Google Maps is contacted only when you choose a map link. Production hosting locations and any required Moroccan cross-border data formalities will be documented before personal-data features are switched on.

6. Cookies and local storage

We currently do not use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. The site stores a language preference locally. Future authentication may use browser storage required to keep a secure session. If we add non-essential analytics, advertising or other cookies involving personal data, we will provide information and obtain consent before placing them where required.

7. Retention

  • Account data: while the account is active or as needed for security/legal obligations.
  • Approved reviews: while useful to the provider profile, unless removal is required or justified.
  • Rejected/pending contributions: only as long as necessary for moderation and abuse prevention.
  • Reports: for the time needed to investigate and document the outcome.
  • Uploaded photos: until removed, rejected or no longer needed for the purpose for which they were submitted.

8. Your rights

Under applicable Moroccan data-protection rules, people may have rights including access, correction and objection. Depending on where you are located, additional rights may apply. Before account features go live we will publish a dedicated privacy-contact method for exercising these rights.

9. Photos and other people

Do not upload photos you do not own or have permission to share. Do not upload sensitive personal information. Photos showing children should only be submitted where the uploader has appropriate parental/guardian permission.

10. Security and changes

Community data is designed to use authenticated accounts, row-level access controls, private photo storage and moderation. No system is risk-free. We may update this policy when the service, law or providers change; the current version will remain available here.

6. ملفات الارتباط والتخزين المحلي

لا نستخدم حالياً ملفات إعلانية أو تتبع سلوكي. نحفظ تفضيل اللغة محلياً. قد يستخدم نظام الدخول مستقبلاً تخزيناً ضرورياً للحفاظ على جلسة آمنة. إذا أضفنا تحليلات أو إعلانات غير ضرورية فسنقدم المعلومات ونطلب الموافقة المسبقة عندما يلزم ذلك.

7. مدة الاحتفاظ

نحتفظ بالبيانات فقط للمدة اللازمة للغرض: الحساب أثناء نشاطه، والمراجعات المعتمدة ما دامت مفيدة، والمساهمات المعلقة أو المرفوضة للمدة اللازمة للمراجعة ومنع الإساءة، والبلاغات حتى انتهاء التحقيق، والصور حتى حذفها أو رفضها أو انتهاء الحاجة إليها.

8. حقوقك

وفقاً للقواعد المغربية المطبقة قد تكون لك حقوق في الولوج والتصحيح والتعرض. وقد تنطبق حقوق إضافية حسب مكان إقامتك. سننشر وسيلة تواصل مخصصة قبل تشغيل الحسابات.

9. الصور وبيانات الآخرين

لا ترفع صورة لا تملكها أو لا تملك إذناً لمشاركتها، ولا ترفع معلومات شخصية حساسة. الصور التي يظهر فيها أطفال يجب أن تكون بموافقة مناسبة من الوالد أو الولي.

10. الأمن والتحديثات

التصميم يعتمد على حسابات موثقة وصلاحيات على مستوى الصفوف وتخزين خاص للصور ومراجعة المحتوى. لا يوجد نظام خالٍ تماماً من المخاطر. قد نحدّث هذه السياسة عندما تتغير الخدمة أو القوانين أو المزودون.
