# Morocco Wedding Guide — Rollout Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Trustable 3-city pilot

Live cities: Khénifra, Fès, Meknès.

Ship and verify:

- premium multilingual UX
- direct local discovery
- quick passwordless account
- authenticated-only reviews/comments/photos/reports
- moderation workflow
- transparent star ranking
- permanent provider profiles
- provider correction/removal process
- privacy/community rules

## Phase 2 — Strong wedding categories in the pilot cities

Add only after research coverage is strong enough:

- venues / salles des fêtes
- photographers & videographers
- Neggafa
- décor & flowers
- orchestra / DJ
- beauty
- cakes / pâtisserie
- wedding planners

## Phase 3 — Major-city expansion

Research and launch one city at a time:

1. Casablanca
2. Rabat
3. Marrakech
4. Tanger
5. Agadir
6. Oujda
7. Tétouan

9. El Jadida

For every new city, require:

- enough real vendors to make the page useful
- working contact data
- category coverage
- unique local copy
- city-specific schema/canonical/sitemap entry
- moderation and correction workflow

## Ranking rules

Default ranking should favor community usefulness, not payment.

Recommended ranking inputs:

1. approved on-site average star rating
2. approved on-site review count
3. listing completeness
4. recent verification date
5. response/contact availability when measurable

A paid or premium listing may receive clearly labelled extra visibility, but payment must never alter the displayed community star score.

## Account model

Guests:

- browse
- search
- filter
- sort
- read approved content
- contact providers

Signed-in members:

- rate once per provider
- write a review/comment
- upload permitted wedding photos
- report incorrect information or inappropriate content

Moderators/admins:

- approve/reject reviews
- approve/reject photos
- process reports
- correct/verify listings

## Domain migration

Do not choose the final domain based only on “caterers.” The product is becoming a broader wedding guide. Once the brand name is chosen, move the GitHub Pages pilot to the final domain with redirects and updated canonical/auth settings.
