Rules for browsing the directory, contacting providers and contributing reviews, photos or reports.

1. What this service is

Morocco Wedding Guide is an independent discovery and community-information platform. We help people find wedding providers and compare public information. Unless clearly stated otherwise, we are not the listed provider, booking agent, payment processor, employer, insurer or guarantor of a provider.

2. Provider information

We research listings from public sources and direct provider information. Details can change. Users must confirm availability, identity, price, menu, capacity, licences where relevant, location, contract terms and payment arrangements directly with the provider before booking.

3. No guarantee or endorsement

A listing does not mean we endorse, certify or guarantee that provider. Labels such as “verified” will only be used when we define and perform a real verification process. Decorative site photography is not a provider portfolio unless clearly identified as such.

4. Rankings and ratings

Community star scores and ranking signals use approved on-site reviews. Businesses begin as “New” until approved feedback exists. Sponsored placement, if introduced, must be clearly labelled and must not secretly alter community star scores.

5. Accounts and contributions

Browsing does not require an account. An account is required for ratings, reviews/comments, photo uploads and reports. You must provide accurate information and keep access to your sign-in email secure. You may not create accounts or submit content to manipulate rankings, impersonate someone, harass others or evade moderation.

6. Review rules

Reviews must reflect a genuine first-hand experience or a direct role in arranging the event. Do not submit fabricated claims, threats, hate, personal attacks, private contact data, medical/financial information or paid promotional material disguised as a review. We may reject, remove or investigate content that violates these rules.

7. Photo rights

By uploading a photo, you confirm that you created it or have sufficient permission to share it and allow us to display it on the service. Do not upload copyrighted work without permission, private documents, sensitive personal information or images of children without appropriate parental/guardian permission. You keep ownership of your content.

8. Reports and corrections

Users and providers may report incorrect details, closures, duplicates, inappropriate content or other issues. Reports are reviewed and are not automatically treated as proof. Providers can request corrections or removal where appropriate.

9. Provider relationships and transactions

Any contract, deposit, payment, cancellation, dispute, service quality issue or event arrangement is between the user and provider unless a future product explicitly states otherwise. Use written contracts and keep payment records for significant bookings.

10. Acceptable use

Do not scrape the service at abusive scale, attack or disrupt it, bypass access controls, upload malware, harvest personal data, spam providers/users or use the platform for unlawful activity.

11. Moderation and suspension

We may moderate submissions, limit features or suspend accounts to protect users, providers and the integrity of rankings. We aim to apply these rules consistently and may allow appeals or corrections where practical.

12. Liability and changes

We work to keep the directory useful and accurate but cannot guarantee uninterrupted access or that every third-party detail is current. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded. We may update these terms as the product changes; the latest version will be published here.
