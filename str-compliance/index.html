Nashville moves STR permit applications online

HIGH
What changed
Nashville introduced a new online short-term rental permit application process effective March 11, 2026.
Who is affected
New STR permit applicants in Nashville.
Action
Use the current online application process and prepare required PDF documents before submission.
Why it matters
A process change can create delays when an operator follows an outdated application workflow or is missing required documentation.
