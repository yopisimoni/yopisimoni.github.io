Live workflow proof

See a property enquiry turn into an instant follow-up workflow.

This demo shows the front door of a lightweight estate-agent automation: a lead is captured, structured, logged and surfaced immediately so the team can respond faster.

01Visitor submits a property or valuation enquiry.
02Make.com receives and structures the lead through a webhook.
03The lead is written into a Google Sheets lead log with a clear status.
04The team receives an immediate Gmail alert with the enquiry details.
Demo only. Please use test information rather than sensitive personal data. The workflow is designed to demonstrate the automation architecture, not to collect real property instructions.

What this proves

A small workflow that can sit behind an existing estate-agency website.

STEP 01

Capture

Connect an existing contact, valuation or landlord form without rebuilding the site.

STEP 02

Route

Send structured fields into Make.com through a webhook and apply business logic.

STEP 03

Log

Keep a searchable lead record in Sheets or extend the workflow into a CRM.

STEP 04

Alert

Notify the right person immediately by email, with WhatsApp/SMS as optional extensions.
