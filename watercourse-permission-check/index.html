Questions before you submit

Does this tell me definitively whether consent is required?

No. We organise the relevant public information, likely route and evidence so you can make a better early-stage decision and confirm the case with the appropriate authority or qualified professional.

Is this an application service?

No. The £49 validation offer is a research and information check. It does not submit an application or prepare engineering calculations.

Why do you ask for the exact site?

The responsible authority, local guidance and applicable process can depend on location. A generic UK guide cannot provide the same site-specific research.

What happens after I submit?

We review whether the request fits the service. If it does, we confirm the scope and payment before beginning. If the project clearly needs specialist engineering or legal advice beyond this service, we will say so rather than oversell the report.
