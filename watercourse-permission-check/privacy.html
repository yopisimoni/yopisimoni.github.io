Privacy Notice

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This notice explains how Watercourse Permission Check handles personal information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, optional telephone number, company or role, site address and postcode, project details, proposed start date, and links or information you choose to provide about plans or documents.

Why we use it

We use this information to assess your enquiry, decide whether the requested site check fits the service, communicate with you about the request, prepare the service if agreed, and maintain appropriate business records.

Service providers

We use third-party infrastructure to receive and store enquiries and deliver email notifications. This currently includes Supabase for enquiry storage, FormSubmit for form email notifications, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Microsoft Outlook for email.

How long we keep information

We aim to keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry, provide any agreed service, maintain appropriate records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Test submissions are removed when no longer required.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you or to request an appropriate correction or deletion. Other rights may apply under UK data protection law depending on the circumstances.

Contact

For privacy questions, email watercoursepermissioncheck@outlook.com.

Important: This notice is intended to describe the current validation service accurately. It should be reviewed if the service, business structure, payment process, analytics, marketing, or data processors change.
