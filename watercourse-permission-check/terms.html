Service Information & Terms

Validation offer — last updated 30 August 2026

What the service is

The Watercourse Permission Check is an independent research and information service. For an accepted site, the service researches publicly available official information relevant to the location and proposed works and organises findings into a concise site-specific report.

What the £49 validation check may cover

Depending on what is publicly available and relevant, the report may identify the likely responsible authority, published permission or consent route, published fees and lead times, supporting-information requirements, local policy notes, source links and matters requiring further confirmation.

Scope confirmation and payment

Submitting the enquiry form does not create an order and no payment is taken by the form. We first review whether the request fits this validation service. If it does, scope and payment arrangements are confirmed before research starts.

Important limitations

The service does not provide legal advice, engineering design, hydraulic calculations, flood-risk advice, planning advice, regulatory approval, or a guarantee that a permission is required, obtainable or unnecessary. Public information can change and site-specific facts may require confirmation by the relevant authority or a suitably qualified professional.

Sources

Reports are intended to identify the official/public sources relied upon and distinguish researched information from matters that require case-specific confirmation.

Customer information

The usefulness of a check depends on the accuracy and completeness of the site and project information supplied. Customers should not provide confidential information that is unnecessary for the initial scope review.

Contact
